About Us

Bridging the Gap Between Classroom Learning and Real-World Digital Skills

iMENA Hub is a Rwandan educational technology initiative dedicated to empowering teachers, learners, and schools through practical digital skills, educational innovation, and modern teaching methods.

Inspired by the meaning of "Imena" — a symbol of excellence, courage, and service — our mission is to help build a generation of competent, creative, and ethical leaders prepared for the future.

We focus on delivering educational technology solutions, coding, AI, and digital literacy to schools and learners across Rwanda and Africa, supporting Rwanda's Vision 2050 and the country's digital transformation.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission for Africa

Leading the transformation of education across Rwanda and the African continent

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading educational technology organizations, transforming how students and teachers engage with learning across the continent. We envision an Africa where every teacher and learner has access to practical digital skills and modern educational tools that unlock their full potential.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world skills by providing practical digital education, teacher development, and educational technology solutions. We deliver hands-on training and innovative EdTech tools to schools and learners across Rwanda and beyond, empowering them for a digital future.

Committed to transforming education through technology, innovation, and practical digital skills while empowering teachers and learners across Rwanda and Africa.

— TUYISENGE Jean Chrisostom, Founder
