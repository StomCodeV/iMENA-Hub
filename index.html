About iMENA Hub

More than a website. A growing technology ecosystem.

iMENA Hub builds digital products and solutions using ICT, AI, EdTech, and web development to solve real problems and create opportunities to learn, work, and innovate.

Rooted in Rwanda, iMENA Hub brings together projects across education, AI, careers, digital skills, and everyday digital needs — with more solutions growing within the ecosystem.
