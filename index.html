About iMENA Hub

Bridging the gap between classroom learning and real-world digital skills through practical education and teacher development.

iMENA Hub is a Rwandan educational technology initiative dedicated to empowering teachers, learners, and schools through practical digital skills, educational innovation, and modern teaching methods.

Our purpose is to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world digital skills through practical education and teacher development programs. We focus on delivering educational technology solutions, coding, AI, and digital literacy to schools and learners across Rwanda and Africa.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become one of Africa's leading educational technology organizations, transforming how students and teachers engage with learning across the continent. Harnessing the power of digital tools, AI, and modern pedagogy to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world readiness.

Mission

To bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world skills by providing practical digital education, teacher development, and educational technology solutions. We deliver hands-on digital skills training, teacher professional development, and innovative EdTech solutions to schools and learners across Rwanda and beyond.
