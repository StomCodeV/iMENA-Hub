Our Philosophy

The Spirit of IMENA

In Rwandan culture, Imena is the category of National Heroes known for their extraordinary sacrifice and service. They are the backbone of our history. We chose this name because we believe software should be as resilient and selfless as our heroes.

King Mutara III Rudahigwa

Symbol of unity and modern progress. He abolished social inequality, just as we aim to abolish digital barriers.

Agathe Uwiringiyimana

A pioneer of democracy and education. Her courage inspires our mission to empower every teacher in Rwanda.

The Vision of StomCodeV

Based in Karumuna Village, Ntarama, I am developing the IMENA Ecosystem to ensure that Rwanda is not just a consumer of tech, but a creator. My aim is to build tools that solve real problems for real people.
